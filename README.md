Developed a comprehensive Supply Chain Dashboard in MS Excel to optimize inventory management and vendor performance. Leveraged the Pareto Principle (80/20 rule) to categorize 100+ SKUs into ABC tiers, identifying the top 20% of products generating 80% of total revenue. Enhanced operational efficiency by analyzing shipping carrier performance (cost vs. lead time) and supplier quality (defect rates). This project provided actionable insights to minimize stockouts of high-value items and reduce logistics costs, demonstrating proficiency in data visualization, PivotTables, and advanced logical formulas.
